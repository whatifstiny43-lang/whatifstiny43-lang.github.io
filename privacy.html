Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how "Shorts Uploader" (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Tiny What Ifs YouTube channel, handles data.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data the App accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and read the status of the channel's own videos.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The App does not collect, sell, or share any personal data, and it does not send data to any server other than Google's APIs.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
